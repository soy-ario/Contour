# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Agency operators (admins) running social media marketing for portfolio clients. They manage content pipelines, client relationships, analytics, and operational workflows daily. Secondary users are the agency's clients, who access a limited portal for content review, performance dashboards, and service requests.

## Product Purpose

Contour is a centralized agency operating system for social media marketing management. It streamlines client onboarding, content creation and approval, social analytics aggregation, and client-agency collaboration. Success means agency teams can manage their entire client portfolio from one place without switching between tools.

## Brand Personality

Confident, clean, premium. Inspired by Linear, Notion, Attio, Stripe, and Vercel — high-craft enterprise SaaS that feels opinionated and refined rather than generic. Every detail should signal quality: generous whitespace, elegant shadows, deliberate typography, and purposeful interactions.

## Anti-references

- No dark mode or terminal aesthetic on content surfaces. The main canvas stays light and airy (the dark navy sidebar is the only dark surface).
- No generic SaaS blue-and-white palettes. The lime accent (#C5F135) is the brand's distinctive signal.
- No glassmorphism, gradient text, or decorative effects — every visual decision serves clarity.
- No over-engineered interactions — motion is purposeful and subtle, not performative.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over density.** Every screen has one primary task. Information is layered — surface the essentials, tuck away the rest.
2. **Premium restraint.** Quality comes from what you omit: fewer borders, generous radii, intentional white space. The interface breathes.
3. **Cards as containers, not clutter.** Cards organize related information without nesting. Each card earns its place.
4. **Typographic hierarchy.** Type is the primary UI element. Weight, size, and spacing do the work of separation — not boxes, not lines.
5. **Consistency by system.** One spacing scale, one radius set, one typeface (Inter), one accent color. Repetition builds trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practices apply: sufficient contrast ratios, keyboard-navigable interfaces, screen-reader-friendly markup. Body text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast requirements. No special accommodations beyond baseline quality.
